Italian Spaghetti

Spaghetti

is one of the most famous Italian dishes.
It’s long pasta usually served with various sauces like tomato or carbonara.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • tomato sauce
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Grated cheese for topping

Steps

  1. Boil spaghetti in salted water until cooked.
  2. Drain the pasta and add olive oil.
  3. Prepare your tomato sauce
  4. Mix spaghetti with the sauce.
  5. Serve hot and sprinkle with grated cheese.
